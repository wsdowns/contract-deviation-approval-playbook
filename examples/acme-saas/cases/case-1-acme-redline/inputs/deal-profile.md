## Why the customer says it needs each change

- Net 45: its controller pays all vendors on 45-day cycles.
- 3% renewal cap: its budget is set annually and a 7% increase would need re-approval.
- 2x liability cap: its counsel wants a cap above 1x on any software contract.
- Removing publicity: it does not allow vendors to name it as a customer.
- 99.9% availability and 15% credits: dispatch runs on the system around the clock.
