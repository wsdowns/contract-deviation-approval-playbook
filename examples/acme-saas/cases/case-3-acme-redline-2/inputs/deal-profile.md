# Deal profile: Summit Ridge Manufacturing

| Field | |
|---|---|
| Customer | Summit Ridge Manufacturing, Inc.; mid-market manufacturer; about 600 employees and 3 plants |
| Deal | New customer. Annual fees $250,000. Term 2 years. Total contract value $500,000. Target signature April 30, 2026, because its current vendor's contract ends May 31 |
| Strategic or standard | **Standard, with upside.** Not a reference account |
| Upside | A successful first plant could extend to the other two plants, roughly $400,000 a year, timing 12 to 18 months |
| Competition | Replacing an incumbent vendor. Two other vendors bid; ACME is the preferred choice |
| Customer data | Production schedules and supplier data. No regulated data |
| If we say no | The seller expects Summit Ridge to drop individual requests but to hold firm on any item its counsel calls a condition of signing |
| Precedent | None known |

## Why the customer says it needs each change

- Quarterly billing in advance: its finance team prefers it to annual prepayment.
- Service credits up to 25% and termination after three missed months: production stops when the system is down.
- Cyber insurance of $10M: its insurer requires vendors to carry that level.
- Removing the cap for gross negligence and willful misconduct: its counsel treats it as a condition of signing.
- Indemnity for ACME's violation of law: its counsel wants it in every software contract.
